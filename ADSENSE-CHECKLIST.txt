MOVVS33 AdSense preparation

1. Upload index.html, about.html, contact.html, privacy.html, terms.html, robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
2. Keep the existing assets/logo.png and assets/favicon.png.
3. Confirm https://movvs33.com/ loads over HTTPS.
4. Confirm every footer policy link works.
5. In Google Search Console, submit https://movvs33.com/sitemap.xml and request indexing for the new pages.
6. Apply for AdSense using https://movvs33.com/.
7. After Google gives you the AdSense verification/ad code, place Google's exact code in the <head> as instructed. Do not invent a publisher ID or ads.txt entry.
8. After approval/setup, add ads.txt using the exact line Google provides in AdSense.
9. For visitors in regions where Google's consent requirements apply, configure a Google-certified CMP through AdSense before serving personalized ads.

Do not add fake articles, copied text, keyword stuffing, fake reviews, or fake traffic to make the site look larger. Keep the content original and accurate.
